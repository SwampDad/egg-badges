# Changelog

## 0.5.0 - 2026-10-24

First public release of the 0.5 series. This follows the 0.2.4 public release.

### Added

- Full-slot background for perfect 6x31-IV eggs in Forest Green, Slate Blue, or Deep Purple.
- Gold, Cyan, and White circle indicators for 5x31 and custom IV filters.
- Compact perfect-IV count with fixed colors.
- Independent gender and confirmed Hidden Ability indicators.
- Settings screen with direct circle-filter activation, preview, and installed-version footer.
- Automatic migration and backup for earlier Egg Badges configurations.

### Changed

- Circle filters activate the circle directly; the separate circle activation checkbox is removed.
- The Custom IV Filter Edit button has its final spacing adjustment.

## 0.2.4 - 2026-10-21

- Initial standalone public release with configurable badges for perfect-IV eggs.
